### Check your microphone

Let's make sure your microphone is connected and working. This only takes a minute.

1. **Plug it in.** If you use an external microphone or a headset, make sure it is connected to your computer. Try a different port if needed.
2. **Check for a mute switch.** Some headsets and microphones have a mute button or a volume dial. Make sure the microphone is not muted.
3. **Test the microphone.** Open your computer's sound settings. Speak and watch for the input level to move. If it moves, the microphone works.
   - **Windows:** Open Settings. Go to System, then Sound. Look under Input.
   - **Mac:** Open System Settings. Go to Sound, then Input.
4. **Check your device's microphone settings.** Your computer has its own setting that controls which apps can use the microphone. Make sure the microphone is turned on and your browser is allowed.
   - **Windows:** Open Settings. Go to Privacy & security, then Microphone.
   - **Mac:** Open System Settings. Go to Privacy & Security, then Microphone.

### Allow microphone access in your browser

Your browser needs your permission to use the microphone. This is a quick fix, and it solves the problem for many people.

1. **Click Allow when asked.** The first time you join the room, your browser will ask to use your microphone. Choose **Allow**.
2. **Pick the right microphone.** If you have more than one microphone, choose the one you want to use. Your browser will remember this choice next time.
3. **Missed the pop-up?** Click the icon to the left of the web address at the top of your browser. Change the microphone setting to **Allow**.
4. **Reload the page.** Your browser may ask you to reload after you change a setting. Reload so the new setting takes effect.

### Close anything else using the microphone

Other apps can block the room from using your microphone. Before you join, close other browser tabs and apps that might be using it. Video call apps and recording tools are common causes. Then reload the page.
