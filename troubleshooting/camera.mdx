title: Camera troubleshooting
description: Camera not working? Try these steps
---
### Check your camera

Let's make sure your camera is connected and working. This only takes a minute.

1. **Plug it in.** If you use an external USB camera, make sure it is connected to your computer. Try a different USB port if needed.
2. **Check for a cover.** Some computers have a built-in shutters or sliders to cover the camera lens. Some people also put tape or a sticker over the lens. Slide the shutter open or remove anything covering the lens.
3. **Test the camera.** Open the camera app on your computer. On Windows, use the Camera app. On macOS, use Photo Booth. If you can see yourself in those apps, the camera works.
4. **Check your device's camera settings.** Your computer has its own setting that controls which apps can use the camera. Make sure the camera is turned on and your browser is allowed.
   - **Windows:** Open Settings. Go to Privacy & security, then Camera. Turn on camera access and make sure your browser is on the list.
   - **Mac:** Open System Settings. Go to Privacy & Security, then Camera. Turn on the switch next to your browser.

### Allow camera access in your web browser

Your browser needs your permission to use the camera. This is a quick fix and solves the problem for many people.

1. **Click Allow when asked.** The first time you log in, your browser will ask to use your camera and microphone. Choose **Allow** for both.
2. **Missed the pop-up?** Click the icon to the left of the web address at the top of your browser. Change the camera and microphone settings to **Allow**.
3. **Reload the page.** Your browser will ask you to reload after you change a setting. Reload so the new setting takes effect.

### Close anything else using the camera

Only one app can use your camera at a time. Before you join the room, close other browser tabs and apps that might be using it. Video call apps are common causes. Then reload the page.
