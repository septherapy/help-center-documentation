title: Connection quality troubleshooting
description: Connection quality not great? Try these recommendations.
---
If the video freezes, the sound cuts out, or people seem to lag behind, your internet connection may be the cause. These steps can help.

### Check your connection

Let's make sure your internet is working. This only takes a minute.

1. **Test your internet.** Open a new browser tab and go to a website like YouTube. If it loads slowly or stops to buffer, your connection is weak.
2. **Check your Wi-Fi signal.** Look at the Wi-Fi icon on your screen. If it shows only one or two bars, move closer to your router.
3. **Run a speed test.** Search for "internet speed test" and run one. For video calls, you want at least 3 Mbps for both download and upload. Faster is better.

### Improve your Wi-Fi signal

Wi-Fi gets weaker with distance and with walls or other barriers in the way. A weak signal causes frozen video and dropped audio.

1. **Stay close to your router.** Stay within 10 to 15 feet of your wireless router. Move closer if there is a wall or divider between you and the router.
2. **Stay in one place.** Do not walk around during the session. Moving between rooms can weaken your signal or drop you from the room.
3. **Use a cable if you can.** A cable from your computer to your router is more stable than Wi-Fi. This is a good choice if you have regular sessions.

### Free up your internet

Everything on your network shares the same connection. Heavy use by other devices can slow your video call.

1. **Pause big downloads and uploads.** Wait until after your session to download files or back up photos.
2. **Stop streaming on other devices.** Ask others in your home or office to pause video, games, and large downloads during your session.
3. **Close extra tabs and apps.** Programs running in the background can use your internet. Close what you do not need.
4. **Turn off your VPN.** A VPN can slow your connection. Turn it off unless you are required to use it.

### Restart your equipment

Restarting often fixes slow or unstable connections.

1. **Restart your router.** Unplug it for 30 seconds. Plug it back in and wait a few minutes until the lights are steady.
2. **Restart your computer.** Then rejoin the room.

### Still having trouble?

Try these last options:

- **Turn off your camera.** Video uses the most internet. Turning it off can keep the audio clear.
- **Switch networks.** If possible, try a different Wi-Fi network or use your phone's hotspot.
