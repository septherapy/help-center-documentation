description: Speakers not working? Try these recommendations.

### Check your speakers

Let's make sure your speakers are on and turned up. This only takes a minute.

1. **Check the connection.** If you use external speakers or a headset, make sure they are plugged in and turned on. Try a different port if needed.
2. **Check the volume.** Make sure your speakers are not muted. Turn the volume up. Some speakers and headsets have their own mute button or volume dial.
3. **Check your computer's volume.** Look for the speaker icon on your screen. Make sure the volume is up and not muted.

### Choose the right speaker

Your computer may be sending sound to the wrong place. This happens often when you switch between headphones and speakers.

1. **Test the sound.** Open a new browser tab and play a video on a site like YouTube. If you hear it, your speakers work.
2. **Pick the correct speaker.** If you hear nothing, open your computer's sound settings. Choose the speaker or headset you want to use.
   - **Windows:** Open Settings. Go to System, then Sound. Look under Output.
   - **Mac:** Open System Settings. Go to Sound, then Output.
3. **Reload the page.** Go back to the room and reload the page. Then test again.

### Keep your volume moderate

A very loud speaker can cause echo. Your microphone picks up the sound and sends it back into the room. Set your volume to a medium level. Headphones also help prevent echo.
