---
title: Using the whiteboard
---
During the session, your provider may turn on the whiteboard for certain activities. When your provider decides to turn on the whiteboard for an activity, you will see a special toolbar at the top-left of your screen.

You can use the mouse to:

- Draw lines and shapes.

- Add text and stamps.

- Change colors and styles for lines, shapes, and text.

Let's go through all the tools you can use!

---

## Drawing tools

When your provider has turned on the whiteboard, you will see a toolbar appear on your screen. While you can see all the buttons on the screen, your provider controls what you can click. They may choose to turn off some features during certain parts of the session so that you can focus on listening or watching. Just follow along and let your provider guide you through the activity!

### Select tool

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/6a7884fd576990b50fa11394bd33f9c4dfb04179/images/Button%20-%20Select%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The select tool helps you move and change items on your whiteboard. You can click any drawing, shape, or text to pick it up.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/4dda3dbc1bcc1f4919b4d1b0b9ab4023ea1d4415/images/Interface%20-%20Select%20tool%20options.svg" width="164" height="84" alt="" style="width: 124; height: 44; float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

When you click on an item using the select tool, a set of extra buttons will appear just below that item. You can use them to:

- **Make copies** - If you want two of the same item, just click this button.

- **Change order** - Sometimes items need to go in front or behind each other. Use these buttons to move things forward or backward so they look right.

- **Clear items** - If you make a mistake, you can delete that item to start again.

The select tool will also allow you to update the style of an item on screen. For example, if you click on a shape, you can use the new menu to change its outline or fill color.

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Moving items">
    To move an item, click and drag it across the whiteboard to its new placement.

    <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/refs/heads/main/images/Interface%20-%20Select%20tool%20move%20object.gif" width="800" height="296" alt="" style="width: 800; height: 296; margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Resizing items">
    You can easily change how big your item looks! To resize an item, find the little dots on it and grab them with your mouse.

    - **Grow evenly:** If you drag a dot in a corner, the item will grow just like it looks now without getting stretched or squished.

    - **Stretch it:** If you drag a dot in the middle of a side, the item will get wider or taller. Use this if you want to change its shape intentionally!

    <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/refs/heads/main/images/Interface%20-%20Select%20tool%20resize%20object.gif" width="529" height="508" alt="" style="width: 529; height: 508; margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />
  </Tab>

  <Tab title="Rotating items">
    You can easily turn your item to face the right way! To rotate an item, grab the **blue arrow** at the top of your shape and drag it around in a circle. The blue arrow always stays attached to the top center of the item. It will not move even if you resize or move the item, making it easy to find wherever you are working!

    <Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/refs/heads/main/images/Interface%20-%20Select%20tool%20rotate%20object.gif" width="389" height="418" alt="" style="width: 389; height: 418; margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

### Pen

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Pen%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The Pen tool is your creative helper! You can draw anything you imagine by clicking and dragging your mouse. To make your drawing stand out, try changing the style of your pen.

- **Pick a color** - Select whatever color matches your idea.

- **Change thickness** - Make big bold lines or small fine details.

- **Try styles** - See how dotted lines or dashed lines can add a fun style to your work.

- **See-through** - You can even make the lines lighter (more transparent) if you want!

To change these settings, click on the pen tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Shapes

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/2cb87bd937f753e29a3aa0aa189d5e1b4063c4f4/images/Button%20-%20Shapes%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0px 0px 15px 15px;" />

You can easily add different shapes to your whiteboard! Pick from squares, circles, stars, and triangles to decorate your work. Once you pick a shape, you have lots of fun ways to change its look.

- **Fill it in** - Choose what color goes inside the shape. You can even make it see-through (faded) if you want.

- **Border options** - Pick the color for the outline and choose how thick or thin it is.

- **Line style** - Try changing the border from a solid line to dotted or dashed lines.

To change these settings, click on the shapes tool in the toolbar, select a shape, and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Arrow

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Arrow%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The arrow tool is a fun way to highlight important parts on the whiteboard! You can add arrows to point at words, shapes, or other drawings.

- **Color it** - Pick whatever color stands out to you.

- **Change thickness** - Make the arrow bold and strong, or keep it simple and thin.

- **Faded look** - If you prefer, you can make the line see-through (faded).

- **Style** - Try changing the lines between solid, dashed, or dotted.

To change these settings, click on the arrow tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Text

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Text%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The text tool is a helpful way to add written information directly onto the whiteboard. You can type words to explain what you see or label parts of your drawings. Customize the way the text looks so it stands out!

- **Pick your look** - Choose the color of your letters. You can even try different fonts so your text looks unique.

- **Make it bold** - Make your writing bigger or add thick lines underneath (underline) for emphasis.

- **Outline fun** - If you want, you can give your words a border to separate them from other drawings.

- **Adjust size** - Make sure everyone can read what you've written by resizing the letters easily.

To change these settings, click on the text tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Stamps

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Stamps%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The stamps tool allows you to place emoji style stamps onto the whiteboard. You can add stamps directly to show what you feel or mean! This is great for sharing concepts that are hard to draw by hand. Pick from a variety of fun groups, such as activities, animals, nature, emotions, and everyday objects.

Once you pick a stamp, click on it to select it again if you want to move or change it. You can also adjust how see-through the image is.

### Erase

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Eraser%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

Sometimes you need to tidy up or fix a small mistake without losing all your hard work! The erase tool lets you remove just the lines, shapes, stamps, or text you don't want anymore.

### Undo and redo

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/4dda3dbc1bcc1f4919b4d1b0b9ab4023ea1d4415/images/Button%20-%20Undo%20and%20redo%20tool.svg" width="90" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

It is okay to make a mistake! Sometimes you want to fix a drawing or decide to try something different. The undo and redo tools are here to help you explore your ideas without feeling stuck.

- **Go back** - If you drew or added something by accident, click undo to take the item away.

- **Try again** - If you change your mind about a correction, use redo to put it back exactly how you had it before.
