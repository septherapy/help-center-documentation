Once you're in the therapy room, you'll see these meeting controls at the bottom of your screen. They're how you get your camera and microphone set up just right before your session starts. Let's take a look at what each button does!

## Microphone controls

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/404af988e0e26faf8fc1ac358348fe5cf84fc4b9/images/Button%20-%20Microphone.svg" width="46" height="46" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0px 0px 15px 15px;" />

Tap this button anytime to mute or unmute your microphone. If your provider says they can't hear you, this is the first place to check!

## Primary camera controls

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/404af988e0e26faf8fc1ac358348fe5cf84fc4b9/images/Button%20-%20Primary%20camera.svg" width="46" height="46" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

This button turns your camera on or off. Keep it on so your provider can see you during your session!

## Document camera controls

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/404af988e0e26faf8fc1ac358348fe5cf84fc4b9/images/Button%20-%20Document%20camera.svg" width="46" height="46" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

If you have a document camera hooked up, use this button to turn it on or off. It's a great way to share a worksheet, book, or drawing with your provider. You can pick which document camera to use in the device settings dialog.

## Device settings

<Image src="https://blob-cdn.documentation.ai/org-2298b9ec-a237-4db7-8e7e-dee229ecc015/doc-f231f1a4-ce9f-444f-aa94-d0fc4be71883/1773181186781-py39bzfdje-Button---Device-settings.Button - Device settings?q=85&fm=auto&auto=compress%2Cformat" width="46" height="46" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

Need to switch things up? Click the gear icon to open your device settings. From here, you can choose which of these to use for your session:

- Primary camera
- Document camera
- Microphone
- Speakers

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  Having trouble getting your camera, microphone, or speakers to work? Check out the [Troubleshooting](/students/troubleshooting) guide for help.
</Callout>

## Leave session

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/404af988e0e26faf8fc1ac358348fe5cf84fc4b9/images/Button%20-%20End%20session.svg" width="46" height="46" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 0;" />

All done for today? Click this button when you're ready to leave your session, and you'll be asked to confirm before you go. Once you leave, you can close your browser tab or window. If you leave by accident, don't worry — you can always rejoin!
