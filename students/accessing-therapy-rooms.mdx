Your school has partnered with Strategic Education Partners (SEP) to provide online therapy services with a provider. Here is how to get connected:

**Get your link:** Your therapist will send you a unique link to join their virtual room.

**Check-in:** Click the link to enter the waiting room. This ensures everything is working properly on your end.

**Turn on your camera and microphone:** When asked, please allow access to your microphone and camera. If you choose "Allow while visiting the site" it will make joining easier next time!

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/refs/heads/main/images/Interface%20-%20Permission%20check.png" width="560" height="558" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

**See yourself:** You should see a video of yourself once permissions are granted.

**Sign in:** Enter your name in the middle of the screen.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/ea77b158037f2f0f9647464195c45b5ea26ce03f/images/Interface%20-%20Waiting%20room%20device%20check.svg" width="500" height="673" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

**Go!** Click **Continue to therapy room** when you are ready. Your therapist will let you into the session shortly.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you are having trouble connecting to your provider's therapy room while in the waiting room, please see the [Troubleshooting](/students/troubleshooting) guide.
</Callout>
