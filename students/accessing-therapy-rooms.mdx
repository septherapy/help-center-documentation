---
title: Accessing therapy rooms
description: Learn how to access your provider's therapy room on the SEP Therapy Platform
---
<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/21680c551b9cda610c1ef8872ac2a5a363a60fec/images/Banner%20-%20Student%20resources.svg" width="640" height="265" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

Your school has partnered with Strategic Education Partners (SEP) to provide online therapy services with a provider. The provider you are working with will send you a unique link to join sessions in their therapy room.

When you click on the link, you will be taken to the waiting room to make sure your computer is configured properly for the session. Your web browser will prompt you to allow access to your microphone and camera.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/refs/heads/main/images/Interface%20-%20Permission%20check.png" width="560" height="558" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

We recommend you choose **Allow while visiting the site** to save your response for future sessions.

After you have granted permission, you should see yourself in your camera.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/ea77b158037f2f0f9647464195c45b5ea26ce03f/images/Interface%20-%20Waiting%20room%20device%20check.svg" width="500" height="673" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

Enter your name in the field in the middle of the screen. When you are ready for the session, click the **Continue to therapy room** button. Your provider will be notified that you are ready and should admit you to the therapy room shortly.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you are having trouble connecting to your provider's therapy room while in the waiting room, please see the [Troubleshooting](/students/troubleshooting) guide.
</Callout>
