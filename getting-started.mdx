#### Having trouble with the platform?

Our Troubleshooting section can help you work through issues but we recommend running either a full session readiness check or core service check before your first session.

<Columns cols="2">
  <Card title="Session readiness check" href="https://app.septherapy.com/session-check" icon="check-circle-icon" cta="Run session readiness check" horizontal="false" target="_blank">
    Our Session readiness check tests your camera, microphone, speakers, and internet connection.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Core services check" href="https://app.septherapy.com/service-check" icon="check-circle-icon" cta="Run core services check" horizontal="false" target="_blank">
