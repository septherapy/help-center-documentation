# **Section 1: Introduction and scope**

Strategic Education Partners ("Company," "we," "us," or "our") operates a web-based telehealth platform that enables licensed mental health professionals to conduct healthcare including both contracted providers and independent providers, to deliver telehealth services, including mental health therapy sessions, to patients in various settings, including school-based settings (the "Platform"). This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information in connection with the Platform, consistent with applicable federal and state privacy laws, including but not limited to the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996 (“HIPAA”), the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health Act (“HITECH”), and applicable state telehealth and data privacy statutes.

This policy applies to the following categories of individuals who interact with the Platform:

- Licensed healthcare providers, including contracted providers and independent providers (collectively, “Providers”), who create accounts and deliver telehealth services through the Platform;
- School districts, healthcare organizations, and other institutional clients and their authorized administrative personnel (“Clients") who access the Platform pursuant to a Client Agreement; and
- Patients, including students (“Patients”), who participate in telehealth sessions through the Platform.

This Privacy Policy does not govern the clinical records, treatment notes, or session notes maintained by Providers in their independent clinical practice or in their capacity as contracted providers. Those records are subject to the Provider’s own notice of privacy practices and applicable law, including HIPAA and HITECH. The Company's role with respect to any protected health information ("PHI") transmitted or stored through the Platform is limited to that of a Business Associate, as described in Section 5 below. To the extent the Company engages contracted providers, additional terms regarding the handling of PHI are set forth in the applicable provider agreement.

By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you are accessing the Platform on behalf of a Client, you represent that you have authority to bind the Client to this policy. Providers acknowledge that their use of the Platform is subject to applicable state telehealth laws, including informed consent requirements, and that they are independently responsible for complying with such laws. In the case of minors who participate, the Provider is obligated to ensure that parental or guardian consent is appropriately obtained. If you do not agree to this policy, do not access or use the Platform.

# **Section 2: Information we collect**

**2.1 Information collected from Providers. &#x20;**&#x57;hen a Provider creates an account on the Platform, we collect the following information:

- Name, email address, used to create and authenticate the account;
- Session metadata, including the start and end times of sessions, the state or jurisdiction from which the Provider connects, and the type of service delivered, associated with the Provider’s account; and
- Communications sent to us by the Provider, including support requests, feedback, and incident reports.

We do not collect or store clinical records, treatment plans, or psychotherapy notes through the Platform. Providers are responsible for maintaining their own clinical documentation systems consistent with applicable professional licensing requirements, HIPAA, and state telehealth recordkeeping laws. Payment information, if collected, is processed through PCI-DSS compliant third-party payment processors and is not stored on the Platform.

**2.2 Information collected from Clients. &#x20;**&#x57;hen a Client executes a Client Agreement, we collect contact information for the Client’s authorized representatives, including name, title, email address, and institutional affiliation. We may also collect information provided in the course of administering the Client Agreement, such as facility names, patient population demographics (in aggregate only), and session scheduling information.

**2.3 Information collected from Patients. &#x20;**&#x54;he information collected from Patients depends on the nature of the telehealth service and the applicable Client Agreement. In school-based settings, Students may access the Platform without creating an account, providing only a display name entered at the time of joining a session. This display name is used solely to facilitate the session and is associated with session metadata (start and end times) for administrative purposes. Students do not provide and we do not collect any other personal information, including date of birth, contact details, or any information that would allow us to identify a Student independently of their display name to facilitate the session. In other settings, Patients or their authorized representatives may provide additional information as required for treatment, payment, or healthcare operations, including name, date of birth, contact information, and insurance information. Any such information constitutes PHI and is handled in accordance with HIPAA, HITECH, and the applicable Business Associate Agreement. We collect only the minimum necessary information required to facilitate the telehealth session and administer the Platform.

**2.4 Automatically collected information. &#x20;**&#x57;hen any user accesses the Platform, we and our third-party analytics providers may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

- Internet protocol (IP) address and approximate geolocation derived from IP address;
- Browser type, version, and device information;
- Pages visited and features accessed within the Platform;
- Session duration and click-path data; and
- Cookie identifiers and similar tracking technologies, as described in Section 7.

This automatically collected information is used for platform analytics, security monitoring, and service improvement. See Section 7 for information about how to manage cookies and tracking preferences.

# **Section 3: How we use information**

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

**Platform operation and administration. &#x20;**&#x57;e use Provider account information and session metadata to authenticate users, facilitate telehealth sessions, verify Provider licensure and credentials, generate administrative records, and support billing and reporting functions under Client Agreements.

**Communications. &#x20;**&#x57;e use Provider and Client contact information to send transactional and operational communications, including account notifications, session confirmations, platform updates, and responses to support inquiries. We do not send marketing or promotional communications without prior opt-in consent.

**Security and fraud prevention. &#x20;**&#x57;e use automatically collected technical information to monitor for unauthorized access, detect security incidents, and maintain the integrity of the Platform.

**Platform improvement. &#x20;**&#x57;e use aggregated and de-identified analytics data to understand how the Platform is used and to improve its features and functionality. De-identification data is data that conforms with legal standards for de-identification. We do not use Patient data, including student data, for platform improvement or product development purposes unless such data has been de-identified in accordance with the foregoing standards.

**Legal compliance. &#x20;**&#x57;e use and retain information to the extent necessary to comply with applicable federal and state law, including our obligations as a Business Associate under HIPAA and HITECH, our obligations to Clients under applicable student privacy laws, state telehealth regulations, and applicable state consumer health data privacy laws.

We do not use any information collected through the Platform to:

- develop or improve commercial products or services for purposes unrelated to the Platform;
- advertise or market to Patients, parents, guardians, or students;
- build individual profiles of Patients or students for any non-treatment purpose; or
- sell, rent, or trade personal information to third parties for their own commercial use.

# **Section 4: Disclosure of information**

We do not sell personal information or PHI. We may share information collected through the Platform in the following limited circumstances:

**Service providers and subcontractors. &#x20;**&#x57;e share information with third-party vendors who assist us in operating the Platform, including cloud infrastructure providers, analytics providers, communication service providers, and payment processors. These vendors are contractually required to process information only on our behalf, only for the purposes described in this policy, and in accordance with applicable law. To the extent any such vendor processes PHI, we have executed a Business Associate Agreement with that vendor consistent with HIPAA and HITECH requirements.

**Clients. &#x20;**&#x57;e share session metadata and administrative reports with the applicable Client as necessary to administer the Client Agreement and support the Client’s oversight of services. Clients receive only the data to which they are entitled under their agreement with us and applicable law. We do not disclose PHI to Clients except as permitted under HIPAA and the applicable Business Associate Agreement.

**Providers. &#x20;**&#x57;e share session metadata and Patient information with the applicable Provider to support their clinical, administrative, and recordkeeping obligations. Contracted providers receive information in accordance with their provider agreement. Independent providers receive information in accordance with the Business Associate Agreement and applicable law.

**Legal obligations and safety. &#x20;**&#x57;e may disclose information when required by law, regulation, or valid legal process, including subpoenas, court orders, or government investigations. We may also disclose information where we have a good-faith belief that disclosure is necessary to prevent imminent harm to any individual, consistent with applicable state mandatory reporting obligations and 45 C.F.R. § 164.512.

**Business transfers. &#x20;**&#x49;n the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of all or substantially all of our assets, user information, including PHI, may be transferred to the successor entity, subject to the successor assuming the obligations of this Privacy Policy, all applicable BAAs, Client Agreements, and provider agreements, and subject to compliance with applicable HIPAA requirements regarding the transfer of PHI in connection with a change of ownership (45 C.F.R. § 164.504(e)(1)(iii)).

We do not disclose information to any other third parties without prior written consent from the applicable user or, in the case of Patient data, from the Patient or their authorized representative (or, for student data, from the applicable Client or parent/guardian as required by law).

# **Section 5: HIPAA, FERPA, and applicable regulatory frameworks**

**5.1  HIPAA and HITECH. &#x20;**&#x54;o the extent that session metadata, Patient information, or other information processed by the Company constitutes protected health information ("PHI") as defined under HIPAA, the Company acts solely as a Business Associate to the applicable Provider, who is the covered entity (or, in the case of contracted providers, may be acting as the workforce member of a covered entity). The Company does not independently determine the purposes or means of processing PHI. The Company complies with the HITECH Act’s requirements applicable to Business Associates, including the Security Rule, the Breach Notification Rule, and applicable provisions of the Privacy Rule that apply directly to Business Associates under 42 U.S.C. § 17934. Providers who use the Platform are responsible for providing patients and guardians with a Notice of Privacy Practices, obtaining any required authorizations for the use of telehealth services, and ensuring compliance with state-specific informed consent requirements for telehealth.

Individuals seeking to exercise their HIPAA rights -- including the right to access, amend, restrict, or request an accounting of disclosures of their PHI -- should contact their Provider directly. The Company will cooperate with Provider requests to retrieve, correct, or restrict PHI held by the Platform in accordance with the terms of the applicable Business Associate Agreement and within the timeframes required by 45 C.F.R. § 164.524 and § 164.526.

**5.2  FERPA. &#x20;**&#x53;ession records maintained by the Company on behalf of a Client that is an educational institution may constitute education records as defined under the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act ("FERPA"), 20 U.S.C. § 1232g. The Company processes such records solely at the direction of the applicable Client and in accordance with the Client Agreement. Educational institution Clients retain responsibility for compliance with FERPA, including providing appropriate notice to parents and guardians and responding to requests for access to education records. Parents and guardians seeking to exercise rights under FERPA should contact their school district directly.

**5.3  State student privacy laws. &#x20;**&#x4D;any states have enacted student data privacy laws that impose additional requirements on educational technology providers. The Company is committed to complying with applicable state student privacy laws in all jurisdictions where it operates in school-based settings. Clients may contact us to request information about our data practices in connection with their specific state law obligations.

**5.4  Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). &#x20;**&#x54;he Platform is not directed to children under the age of 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13 for the Company's own commercial purposes. In school-based settings, student access to the Platform is provisioned through Clients, and the information collected is limited as described in Section 2.3. To the extent the Company collects personal information from children under 13 in connection with telehealth services, such collection is performed with the consent of the child’s parent, guardian, or authorized school official, as required by COPPA (16 C.F.R. Part 312). If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected personal information from a child under 13 without proper consent, we will take prompt steps to delete that information.

**5.5  State telehealth laws. &#x20;**&#x54;he Company operates in compliance with applicable state telehealth laws and regulations in each jurisdiction where Providers deliver services through the Platform. These laws may impose requirements regarding informed consent, provider licensure, prescribing practices, patient location documentation, and recordkeeping. Providers are independently responsible for complying with the telehealth laws of the state in which the Provider practices and the state where the Patient is located at the time of the session. The Company supports Provider compliance by collecting and verifying licensure information and maintaining records of session jurisdiction as described in Section 2.

**5.6  State consumer health data privacy laws. &#x20;**&#x43;ertain states have enacted consumer health data privacy laws that may apply to health-related information not otherwise governed by HIPAA. To the extent any information collected through the Platform constitutes “consumer health data” under such laws, the Company will comply with applicable consent, access, deletion, and data sharing requirements. The Company does not sell or share consumer health data for advertising purposes.

# **Section 6: Data retention and security**

**6.1  Retention. &#x20;**&#x57;e retain personal information and PHI (if applicable) for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, to administer active Client Agreements and Provider accounts, and to comply with applicable legal obligations, including HIPAA’s minimum retention requirements and state medical recordkeeping laws. Session metadata is retained in accordance with the terms of applicable Business Associate Agreements and Client Agreements. PHI is retained for a minimum of six (6) years from the date of creation or the date when it was last in effect, whichever is later, unless a longer retention period is required by applicable state law or Client Agreement. When data is no longer required, we delete or de-identify it in accordance with our internal data retention policy.

**6.2  Security. &#x20;**&#x57;e implement and maintain reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards that comply with the HIPAA Security Rule (45 C.F.R. Part 160 and Part 164, Subparts A and C) and are designed to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. These measures include, but are not limited to: role-based access controls; encryption of PHI in transit and at rest; audit logging of all access to PHI; regular risk assessments consistent with 45 C.F.R. § 164.308(a)(1)(ii)(A); workforce training on privacy and security; and business continuity and disaster recovery procedures. Telehealth sessions are conducted using end-to-end encrypted communication channels. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted to or from the Platform.

**6.3  Breach notification. &#x20;**&#x49;n the event of a security incident affecting personal information subject to applicable Florida law or breach of unsecured PHI (as defined in 45 C.F.R. § 164.402), the Company will notify affected Therapists and Districts in accordance with applicable law and the terms of any executed Business Associate Agreement or District Agreement. Notification timelines and procedures are governed by those agreements and by applicable federal and state. For non-PHI personal information, we will notify affected Clients in accordance with applicable state breach notification laws, including Florida Statutes § 501.171. The Company maintains a written incident response plan that governs its breach identification, investigation, containment, and notification procedures.

# **Section 7: Cookies and third-party analytics**

**7.1  Cookies. &#x20;**&#x54;he Platform uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to support authentication, remember user preferences, and collect analytics data. Cookies are small text files stored on your browser or device. We use the following categories of cookies:

- Strictly necessary cookies: required for the Platform to function, including session authentication. These cannot be disabled without impairing Platform functionality.
- Analytics cookies: used by us and our third-party analytics providers to understand how users interact with the Platform, measure performance, and improve features. These cookies collect information such as pages visited, session duration, and click behavior.\


**7.2  Third-party analytics providers. &#x20;**&#x57;e use third-party analytics tools, which may include services such as Google Analytics, to collect and analyze usage data on non-clinical pages of the Platform. These providers may collect information about your use of the Platform using their own cookies and tracking technologies, subject to their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party analytics providers we use. We do not permit our analytics providers to use data collected through the Platform for their own advertising or commercial purposes.

**7.3  Managing cookies. &#x20;**&#x59;ou may configure your browser to block or delete cookies. Blocking strictly necessary cookies will impair your ability to use the Platform. Blocking analytics cookies will not affect your ability to access the Platform's core features. Opt-out mechanisms provided by third-party analytics providers, such as the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on, may also be available.

**7.4  Do Not Track. &#x20;**&#x53;ome browsers transmit a "Do Not Track" signal. The Platform does not currently respond to  Do Not Track signals. We will update this policy if our practices change.
