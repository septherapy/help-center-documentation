---
title: Firewall settings
---
The SEP Therapy Platform is accessed entirely through a web browser with no additional software installation required. However, to ensure therapists and students can connect successfully, your network firewall must be configured to allow traffic on specific ports. Please review the requirements below and ensure these are in place before any sessions are scheduled.

## Required domains

If your filtering system requires exact hostnames rather than wildcards, contact [support@septherapy.com](mailto:support@septherapy.com) for the current specific hostnames.&#x20;

| Protocol | Ports | Destination       | Explanation                               |
| -------- | ----- | ----------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.septherapy.com | Core application access                   |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.googleapis.com | Authentication and data storage           |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.firebaseio.com | Realtime session state and presence       |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.livekit.cloud  | Live video and audio for therapy sessions |

Opening these domains at the firewall level is necessary but not always sufficient. A few things we see districts miss:

- **SSL/TLS inspection.** If your network uses an SSL-decrypting proxy or inspection appliance, add the domains above to its inspection bypass list in addition to any firewall allow rule. Decrypting inspection can interfere with video sessions even when the domain itself is permitted.
- **WebSocket support.** Real-time session updates and video signaling rely on WebSocket connections and not just standard HTTPS requests. Confirm your firewall or proxy permits the WebSocket upgrade handshake on these domains rather than only plain HTTP(S) traffic.
- **Content filtering products.** If your district uses a separate K-12 content filter alongside your network firewall, these often block by content category rather than by domain or port. Video conferencing and analytics traffic can be caught by category filters even when your firewall is correctly configured. We recommend you add the domains above to your content filter's allowlist as well.
- **Connection idle timeouts.** Therapy sessions typically run 30–60 minutes. If your firewall has a short idle-connection timeout, it can drop an active session's connection mid-call. Confirm the timeout for these domains is long enough to cover a full session.

If sessions are connecting but audio or video is unreliable after confirming the above, please contact us at [support@septherapy.com](mailto:support@septherapy.com) so we can diagnose it further.
