---
title: Word Guess
---
Take turns guessing the word one letter at a time. Right letters help the bunny hop closer to its burrow while wrong ones make it step back. Fill in the word to get the bunny home!

Word Guess is played by 1 to 6 players.

---

## How to play Word Guess

### The goal
- Guess the secret word together to help the bunny hop home to its burrow.

### Guessing
- Take turns. Whoever's card is lit picks a letter, then the turn passes to the next player.
- Tap a letter you think is in the word, or press it on your keyboard.

### The bunny
- If the letter is in the word, it shows up and the bunny hops 1 stone closer to home.
- If it is not, the bunny steps 1 stone back.

### The end
- Fill in every letter and the bunny hops into its burrow!
- If the bunny gets back to the first stone it lies down for a rest. The word is shown and you can try a new one.
