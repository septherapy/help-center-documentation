title: Ludo
---
Get all of your tokens around the board and home. Watch out for other players who can send you back!

Ludo is played by 2 to 4 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Ludo

### The goal
- Be the first player to get all of your tokens home.

### Starting out
- All of your tokens start in your yard.
- Roll a 6 to bring 1 token out onto the path.
- No 6? Your turn is over. The next player goes.

### On your turn
- Tap the die to roll it.
- Move a token forward that many squares.
- Have more than 1 token out? Tap the one you want to move.

### Rolling a 6
- A 6 brings a token out of the yard.
- A 6 also gives you another turn.

### Sending tokens back
- Land on another player's token and it goes back to its yard.
- Squares with a star are safe. No one can be sent back from a star.

### Getting home
- Go all the way around the board, then up your own colored path to the middle.
- You need the exact number to land in the middle.

### Winning
- The first player with all of their tokens home wins!
