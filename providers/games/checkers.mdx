---
title: Checkers
---
Move your pieces across the board and jump over the other player's pieces to capture them. Reach the far side to make a king!

Checkers is played by 2 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Checkers

### The goal
- Capture all of the other player's pieces, or block them so they cannot move.

### On your turn
- Tap one of your pieces. Then tap where you want it to go.
- A piece moves one square forward, on a slant.

### Jumping
- If a piece of the other color is next to yours, and the square behind it is empty, you can jump over it and capture it.
- If you can jump, you must jump.
- Keep jumping if you can!

### Kings
- A piece that reaches the far side becomes a king.
- A king can move forward and backward.

### Winning
- You win when the other player has no pieces left, or no moves.
- If no one captures for a very long time, the game is a draw.
