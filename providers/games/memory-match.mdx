---
title: Memory Match
---
Take turns flipping over cards to match the pictures. Find a pair and go again!

Memory Match is played by 1 to 6 players.

---

## How to play Memory Match

### The goal
- Find the most pairs of matching pictures.

### Your turn
- Take turns. Whoever's card is lit turns over two cards.
- If the two pictures match, you keep the pair and go again.
- If they do not match, everyone gets a look, then they turn back over and the next player goes.

### Remembering
- Try to remember where each picture is. Cards stay in the same place all game.

### The end
- When every pair is found, the player with the most pairs wins.
- If you have the same number, it is a tie.
