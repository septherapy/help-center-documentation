---
title: Dig for Worms
---
Hide 4 worms in your garden, then take turns digging for the other player's worms.

Dig for Worms is played by 2 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Dig for Worms

### The goal
- Dig up all of the other player's hidden worms before they dig up yours.

### Hiding your worms
- You have 4 worms: 3, 4, 5 and 6 squares long.
- Pick a worm, then tap your garden to put it there. Use the turn button beside a worm to lie it across or stand it down. Tap a worm in your garden to pick it up, then tap a new spot to move it. Worms cannot overlap.
- Shuffle hides them all in random places. Press Done when you are happy.

### Digging
- Take turns digging a square in the other player's garden.
- Tap a square in the other player's garden.
- You will see just soil, or a worm.

### Finding worms
- If you find part of a worm, you dig again. Try the squares next to it to follow the worm along.
- When you have dug up every square of a worm, it wiggles into your bucket.

### Winning
- The first player to find all of the other player's worms wins!
