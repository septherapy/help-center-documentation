---
title: Tic-tac-toe
---
Take turns putting your X or O. Get 3 in a row, across, up and down, or diagonally to win!

Tic-tac-toe is played by 2 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Tic-tac-toe

### The goal
- Be the first player to get 3 of your marks in a row.

### On your turn
- Tap an empty square to put your mark in it.
- One player is X and the other is O. X goes first.
- Then it is the other player's turn.

### 3 in a row
- 3 in a row can go across, up and down, or on a slant.
- Block the other player when they have 2 in a row!

### Winning
- The first player to get 3 in a row wins!
- If the board fills up with no 3 in a row, it is a draw.
