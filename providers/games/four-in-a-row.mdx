---
title: Four in a Row
---
Take turns dropping colored discs into the board. The first player to line up 4 in a row wins!

Four in a Row is played by 2 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Four in a Row

### The goal
- Be the first player to get 4 of your discs in a row.

### On your turn
- Tap a column.
- Your disc drops down to the lowest empty spot.
- Then it is the other player's turn.

### 4 in a row
- 4 in a row can go across, up and down, or on a slant.
- Block the other player when they have 3 in a row!

### A full column
- A column that is full cannot be used. Pick another one.

### Winning
- The first player to line up 4 discs wins!
- If the board fills up with no 4 in a row, it is a draw.
