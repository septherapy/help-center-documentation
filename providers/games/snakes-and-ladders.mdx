---
title: Snakes and Ladders
---
Race your token to the last square! Ladders move you up the board while snakes slide you back down.

Snakes and Ladders is played by 1 to 4 players. It can be played against the computer.

---

## How to play Snakes and Ladders

### The goal
- Be the first player to reach the last square.

### On your turn
- Tap the spinner to spin it.
- Your token moves forward the number it lands on, from 1 to 8.
- Then it is the next player's turn.

### Ladders
- Land on the bottom of a ladder and you climb up to the top!

### Snakes
- Land on a snake's head and you slide down to its tail.

### Reaching the end
- You need the exact number to land on the last square.
- Spin a number that is too high? Your token stays where it is and your turn is over.

### Winning
- The first player to land on the last square wins!
