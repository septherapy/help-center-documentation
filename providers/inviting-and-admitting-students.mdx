## Inviting students to your therapy room

Students can access your therapy room through your unique link which can be found on your dashboard or within the therapy room itself. Your unique link is made up of your username and is based on your first and last name by default.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/5193f946c0e02e6e23f00479c1b479c864eb3d3e/images/Interface%20-%20Dashboard%20invite.svg" width="720" height="126" alt="" style="margin: 0 auto; cursor: pointer;" />

You have the ability to copy the link to your clipboard or to send an automated email to participants.

## Waiting room placement

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/9628fee388acd93d7fdd6a2d20bfd46343bb98a9/images/Interface%20-%20Waiting%20room.svg" width="328" height="207" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

When a student clicks your link, they are not immediately placed in your therapy room. Instead, they enter a waiting room where they enter their name and can check their camera and microphone before joining you. You will receive a notification whenever a new participant requests access. Where you see this notification depends on your current location:

- **If you are outside the therapy room** - A notification will appear as a popup in the bottom right hand side and on your dashboard. Clicking this notification will take you directly to the room to manage the request.

- **If you are already in the therapy room** - A notification will appear as a popup in the bottom right hand side. Clicking the notification open the participants panel where you can admit the student.

When students first enter your waiting room, they will be identified as "New participant". This label will update to their name once they have entered one. You will also be able to see whether their camera and microphone are functioning properly and if they currently have their camera off or microphone muted.

Once a student confirms they are ready for the session, you will be able to admit or decline their request.

## Admitting or declining entry

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/ba233f99c5d704763db1ddd692c4721fa01e1514/images/Interface%20-%20Student%20record%20selection.svg" width="296" height="451" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

Once a student is in the waiting room, you have the authority to either admit them to the session or decline their request.

### **To admit a student**

Admitting a student requires linking them to a student record. This step is necessary to accurately log the start and end times of the session. See <a href="/providers/student-records" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Student records</a> for additional details.

- **Select a student record** - When you click to admit a student, a list of existing student records will appear. Select the correct student.

- **Create a new student record&#x20;**- If the student does not have an existing record, you can create one on the spot. You will simply need to enter their name, date of birth, and choose their school.&#x20;

Once confirmed, the student will move into the current session section on the participants panel and their video display box will become visible (see <a href="/providers/participant-video-display" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Participant video display</a> for additional details). &#x20;

### **To decline a student**

If you click to decline a request, the student will be sent back to the waiting room. They retain the ability to request access again if they need to re-attempt the connection.
