---
title: Ending sessions
---
Once you have selected a student record and admitted a student to your therapy room, the entry time is logged to begin the session. Sessions can be ended either by dismissing individual students or by ending the session by clicking the end session button in the meeting controls toolbar. Additionally, if a student closes their web browser tab or window and does not return for 90 seconds, they will be considered dismissed from the session. 

When a session is complete, the dismissal time for the student will be logged to complete session tracking. The session duration will be available on the student record (see <a href="/providers/student-records" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Student records</a> for additional details).

## Dismissing students

Students can be dismissed individually by selecting the **Dismiss participant** option from the participant options menu on the video display box or within the participants panel. You will be prompted to confirm dismissing the student.

You can dismiss multiple students by clicking on the end session button in the meeting controls toolbar (see [Meetings controls](/providers/meeting-controls) for additional details). When you end the session, you will be presented with the two options:

- Stay in therapy room - All participants will be dismissed from the session while you remain in the therapy room. Primarily used to end the current session and admit a participant to start a new session.

* End session and exit - You and all participants will be dismissed from the session. Providers will be redirected to their dashboard while students will be redirected to a post-session page.

If you close your browser tab or window, participants will remain in the therapy room for 90 seconds after which they will be automatically dismissed with the session considered complete. This is to allow you to rejoin the session in case of minor technical difficulties without having to have students rejoin the session.&#x20;
