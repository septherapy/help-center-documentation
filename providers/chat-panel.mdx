---
title: Text chat
---
Text chat lets providers and students send written messages to each other during a session. The text chat panel can be used to spell out a word, confirm an instruction, or check in without interrupting the spoken conversation. Messages are visible only to the people currently in the room.&#x20;

## Opening and closing the chat panel

To open the text chat panel, click the **Text chat** button in the bottom toolbar. The panel appears on the right side of the therapy room and the **Text chat** button will remain highlighted. Click the button again to close the panel. Closing the panel does not leave the chat or prevent you from receiving messages.&#x20;

You and everyone else in the session can open or close the chat panel independently. If you close yours, others still see their chat if theirs is open.

## Sending messages

Text entered in the text chat panel appear in the conversation thread with your name and the time it was sent. Messages from other participants appear in the same thread so everyone sees one shared conversation.

If someone sends a message while your chat panel is closed a green dot appears on your **Text chat** button and a short chime plays to alert you.

## How long messages last

Chat messages are limited to the current session and to the people in the therapy room at the time. Only participants who are in the therapy room when a message is sent will see it. If someone leaves and rejoins or joins later, they will not see messages sent while they were away.

Because of this, chat is not a good place for information someone needs to keep or refer back to later. We recommend you share anything important or that needs to be referred to later through your regular communication channels.

## Provider controls

Providers have additional options to manage the experience.

### **Text size**

The **+** and **−** buttons next to the _Text size&#x20;_&#x6C;abel allow you increase or decrease the font size of the messages. This setting applies to all participants in the session so it's the right tool when a student needs larger text.&#x20;

### **Deleting the chat**

Accessible from the more options menu, the **Clear all messages** option will remove all messages in the thread. Clearing removes the messages for everyone in the room and cannot be undone. Download the transcript first if you need a record of it.

### Saving the chat

Accessible from the more options menu, the **Download conversation** option will allow to save a copy of the messages.&#x20;
