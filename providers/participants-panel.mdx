## Accessing the participants panel

The participants panel can be accessed by clicking the Participants button in the top toolbar. The panel can be closed by clicking the button again or by clicking the X button in the right hand corner.

The participants panel is split into three areas:

### Waiting room

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/9628fee388acd93d7fdd6a2d20bfd46343bb98a9/images/Interface%20-%20Waiting%20room.svg" width="328" height="207" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

Students joining a session in your therapy room will be placed in your waiting room where they can confirm their devices are properly setup and enter their name before requesting to be admitted. You can admit them to your therapy room by selecting a student record that matches the incoming participant (see [Student records](/providers/student-records) and [Admitting students](/providers/admitting-students) for additional details).

### Current session

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/9628fee388acd93d7fdd6a2d20bfd46343bb98a9/images/Interface%20-%20Current%20session.svg" width="328" height="239" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

All participants currently in the therapy room will be listed here. By default, you will see your name here before any other participants join the session.

For other participants, you can access additional options such as asking them to unmute their microphone, hiding or showing their primary camera display, preventing or allowing mouse clicking for activities, changing their display label, or dismissing them from the session. These options are also available from the participant options menu within each participant video display (see [Participant video display](/providers/participant-video-display) for additional details).&#x20;

### Invite participants to this session

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/5193f946c0e02e6e23f00479c1b479c864eb3d3e/images/Interface%20-%20Invite%20participants.svg" width="328" height="184" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

Your unique therapy room link will be displayed here along with buttons to copy the link to your clipboard or email the link to participants.
