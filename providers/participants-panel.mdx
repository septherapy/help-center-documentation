---
title: Participants panel
---
The participants panel allows you to manage students waiting to join, view active participants, and share your session link. To open the participants panel, click the **Participants** button located in the top toolbar. To close the panel, click the **Participants** button again or click the **X** in the top right corner of the panel.

---

## **Panel overview**

The participants panel is divided into three distinct sections:

### **Waiting room**

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/9628fee388acd93d7fdd6a2d20bfd46343bb98a9/images/Interface%20-%20Waiting%20room.svg" width="328" height="207" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

Students will arrive in your waiting room before they are admitted to the session. This area allows students to enter their names and test their camera and microphone.

When a student requests to join, their name will appear in the waiting room section. To admit them, click admit and select the student record that matches the incoming participant. For more help, see <a href="/providers/student-records" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Student records</a> and <a href="/providers/admitting-students" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Admitting students</a>.

### **Current session**

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/9628fee388acd93d7fdd6a2d20bfd46343bb98a9/images/Interface%20-%20Current%20session.svg" width="328" height="239" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

This section lists everyone currently active in your therapy room. When you first enter the room, you will see your own name listed here.

As students are admitted to the therapy room, you can manage their experience directly from this list. From the options menu to the right of their name, you can:

- **Mute/unmute** - Ask a student to unmute their microphone.

- **Camera control** - Hide or show a student's primary camera display.

- **Activity control** - Enable or prevent mouse clicking during interactive activities.

- **Labels** - Change the display name (label) of a participant.

- **Dismiss** - Remove a participant from the session.

These controls can also be accessed through the options menu located on each student's individual video display. see <a href="/providers/participant-video-display" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Participant video display</a> for additional details.

### **Invite participants**

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/5193f946c0e02e6e23f00479c1b479c864eb3d3e/images/Interface%20-%20Invite%20participants.svg" width="328" height="184" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 25px 25px; cursor: pointer;" />

This section is used to share your session with others. Your unique Therapy Room link is displayed here. You can:

- **Copy link** - Click the **Copy** **link&#x20;**&#x62;utton to save the link to your clipboard (to paste into an email or chat).

- **Email link** - Click the **Invite by email&#x20;**&#x62;utton to send the link directly to participants via email.
