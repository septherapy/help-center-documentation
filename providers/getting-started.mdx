<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete account setup" title-type="h2">
    Once an account for the SEP Therapy Platform has been created, we will send you an email with instructions on how to access the platform. Please read through the email and complete the process for signing up for the platform.

    We recommend using your Google account associated with your Strategic Education Partners email address so that you don't need to remember another password to access the Therapy Platform.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Sign in to the platform" title-type="h2">
    After you have signed up, you will arrive on your dashboard. Your dashboard contains your unique link for inviting participants to your therapy room, your waiting room notifications, and links to help and support resources.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add your caseload" title-type="h2">
    The platform uses student records to track session duration. We recommend setting up your caseload by creating student records for each student ahead of your first session (see [Student records](/providers/student-records) for additional details).
  </Step>

  <Step title="Familiarize yourself with your therapy room" title-type="h2">
    Either from your dashboard or by clicking on the therapy room link in the navigation, go check out your therapy room so you are prepared for your first session. The [Therapy room](/providers/therapy-room) platform guide has more details about all the features in the therapy room.
  </Step>
</Steps>

That's it! You're now ready to conduct services with students using the SEP Therapy Platform.
