The therapy room is your dedicated virtual space for conducting student sessions. Each SEP provider is assigned a unique link used to invite students to their therapy room.

## **Finding and sharing your session link**

To invite students to a session, you must provide them with the link to your therapy room. You can locate this link on the dashboard or in the participants panel within the therapy room itself.&#x20;

## **How to enter your therapy room**

You can enter your session via the main navigation menu or from the dashboard.

When you first access the Therapy Room, your web browser will request permission to use your camera and microphone. You can select the option to save your response so that your browser remembers these permissions for future sessions.&#x20;

## **Verifying your audio and video**

Once you enter the room, a preview screen will appear. This screen allows you to confirm that your devices are functioning correctly. If you do not see your camera or if the audio is not working, click the gear icon to open device settings. From here, you can select the correct camera or microphone from the dropdown menu. You can also select a document camera if you have one.

Once you have confirmed everything is working, click the **Continue to therapy room** button to begin your session.
