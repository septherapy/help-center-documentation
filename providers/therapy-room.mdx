The therapy room is your dedicated virtual space for conducting student sessions. We recommend familiarizing yourself with your therapy room ahead of your first session. While you can access your therapy room at any time, students will need to be invited and admitted for their sessions. You can find your link on the dashboard or in the participants panel within the therapy room itself.

## How to access your therapy room

You can access your therapy room via the main navigation menu or from the dashboard. When you first enter your therapy room, your web browser will request permission to use your camera and microphone. You can select the option to save your response so that your browser remembers these permissions for future sessions.

After the permission check, a preview screen will appear. This screen allows you to confirm that your devices are functioning correctly. If you do not see your camera or if the audio is not working, click the gear icon to open device settings. You can also select a document camera if you have one.

Once you have confirmed everything is working, click the **Continue to therapy room** button.

## Sections of the therapy room

The therapy room is divided into three major sections:

### Top toolbar

Provides access to the platform navigation and panel activation buttons.

Relevant help articles:

- [Participants panel](/providers/participants-panel)

- [Activity panel](/providers/activity-panel)

- [Chat panel](/providers/chat-panel) (coming soon)

### Participant video display and workspace

Shows the video feeds from all participants in the therapy room along with the activity workspace if activated.

Relevant help articles:

- [Participant video display](/providers/participant-video-display)

- [Activity panel](/providers/activity-panel)

### Meeting controls toolbar

Contains device controls for each participant to manage their audio and video during the session.

Relevant help article:

- [Meeting controls](/providers/meeting-controls)
