The activities panel allows you to access various activities that can be used with students during your session. To open the activities panel, click the **Activities** button located in the top toolbar. To close the panel, click the **Activities** button again or click the **X** in the top right corner of the panel.

---

## **Panel overview**

The activities panel is your hub for managing what you're doing in a session. It's divided into two sections.

### Currently open activities

This section shows any activities you're actively using. Click on an activity here to bring it back into focus.

### Available Activities

You have three activities to choose from:

<Card title="My content" horizontal="false" target="_self">
  Upload and display images or PDFs. You can view them on their own or annotate them with the whiteboard. Learn more in the [Adding content](/providers/whiteboard#adding-content) and [Accessing the whiteboard](/providers/whiteboard#accessing-the-whiteboard) sections in the main Whiteboard article.
</Card>

<Card title="Screen sharing" horizontal="false" target="_self">
  Share your screen or a specific window with your students. Learn more in Screen sharing article.
</Card>

<Card title="New Card" horizontal="false">
  Use an interactive collaborative whiteboard with your students. Learn more in the [Whiteboard](/providers/whiteboard) article.
</Card>
