title: Activities
---
The Activities panel lets you bring interactive tools into a therapy session so they're visible to everyone in the room. You can control exactly what participants see at any moment, and pause an activity without losing your progress.

To open the activities panel, click the **Activities** button in the top navigation bar. The panel has two sections: **Currently open activities**, which lists anything that's running but minimized, and **Activities**, which lists the tools available to start (Screen Share, Whiteboard, My Documents).

## **Starting an activity**

Select an activity from the list to view its details. Once an activity has been started, the activity opens in the main view with its own toolbar.

### **Toggling visibility**

Active activities includes a visibility control in the toolbar which reads either **Visible to participants** or **Hidden from participants**. Clicking it switches between the two states. This lets you prepare or adjust an activity (for example: setting up a document on a whiteboard) before revealing it to the participant without closing or losing the activity.

### **Minimizing an activity**

Click the minimize icon (the inward-pointing arrows) in the activity's toolbar to hide it from view for everyone while preserving its current state, similar to minimizing an application window on a computer. Once minimized, the activity moves into the **Currently open activities** section of the Activities panel, and the count next to that heading increases.

### **Restoring a minimized activity**

Open the Activities panel and find the activity listed under **Currently open activities**. Click the expand icon next to it to bring the activity back into the main view, exactly as it was left.

### **Closing an activity**

Closing an activity (the red X icon) is different from minimizing it. Closing permanently ends the activity and erases its content — when you close a whiteboard, for example, a confirmation prompt warns that this action cannot be undone. Use minimize if you want to pause an activity and preserve it, and only use close when you're finished with it for good.
