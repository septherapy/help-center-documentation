## Accessing the whiteboard

Click the **Activities** button in the top toolbar to open the activities panel. From here, you have two options: start with a blank whiteboard or use your uploaded content.

<Card title="Starting with a blank whiteboard" horizontal="false" target="_blank">
  Select Whiteboard in the Activities section and then click the **Start whiteboard** button. The whiteboard will open in the workspace and you'll see the **Annotate** button on the left. Click the button to access the whiteboard tools.
</Card>

<Card title="Using the whiteboard with your content" horizontal="false" target="_self">
  Select My content in the Activities section. You'll see a list of all the files you've uploaded. Find the one you'd like to work with and click on it to see its details. Next, hover over the thumbnail and click Display. Your content will now appear in the workspace. Finally, click the **Annotate** button on the left side to reveal the whiteboard tools. To learn more about uploading your content, please see the [Adding content](#adding-content) section below.
</Card>

Once you activate the whiteboard, specific tools will appear on your screen to manage the session environment. As the provider, your access differs from the student's access:

- **Whiteboard control toolbar** - Visible and interactive only for you as the provider. Students cannot see or use this area.&#x20;

- **Drawing tools** - Visible and interactive for both you and students. Use these for shared activities like drawing or writing.

## Whiteboard control toolbar

The whiteboard control toolbar is visible only to providers and allows you to manage sheets, the background of the whiteboard, positioning and zooming, and visibility for other participants in the therapy room.

### Sheets

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/48acf7cca29f85276c992a8b8921ab3b71235f34/images/Button%20-%20Whiteboard%20sheets.svg" width="95" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px; border-radius: 0px" />

Use the sheets tool to organize multiple pages of whiteboard content. This allows you to separate whiteboard sheets for multiple students or to use different sheets with different PDF pages (see using the whiteboard with other activities below for additional details).&#x20;

If this is your first time using the whiteboard, you will start with a blank default sheet.

### Backgrounds

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/48acf7cca29f85276c992a8b8921ab3b71235f34/images/Button%20-%20Whiteboard%20background.svg" width="126" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

Use the background tool to manage the visual appearance of your whiteboard. You can select from various patterns to set a calm environment for the session.

- **Solid colors** - Use a single color fill to create a uniform look. Adjust the transparency of colored backgrounds to control how visible the background is compared to your drawing content.

- **Patterns** - Select options like dots, grids, or lines to organize space visually.

### Positioning and zoom controls

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/48acf7cca29f85276c992a8b8921ab3b71235f34/images/Button%20-%20Whiteboard%20positioning.svg" width="46" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

Use the positioning tool to move the whiteboard canvas independently of your content. This is useful for aligning elements or adjusting the view after you have changed the zoom level. To move the board, click the positioning button and drag the entire whiteboard frame to fit your needs.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/48acf7cca29f85276c992a8b8921ab3b71235f34/images/Button%20-%20Whiteboard%20zoom%20controls.svg" width="156" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The zoom controls allow you to zoom in and out of the whiteboard to make objects larger or smaller respectively.&#x20;

- **Zoom in/out** - Use the **+ (plus)** and **– (minus)** buttons to enlarge or reduce the view of objects.

- **Check level** - The current zoom percentage is displayed on the screen for easy reference.

- **Reset view** - Click the reset button to return the zoom level to 100%.

### Visibility

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/48acf7cca29f85276c992a8b8921ab3b71235f34/images/Button%20-%20Whiteboard%20visibility.svg" width="186" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

As the provider, you control what students see at any moment. Use the visibility button to prepare your workspace in private before bringing it into the shared space. This ensures that students remain focused on the present activity rather than getting distracted by new content or layouts.

- **Visible to participants** - Everyone in the session will be able to see the contents of the whiteboard.

- **Hidden from participants** - Only you will be able to see what is on the whiteboard. Students will see a blank white screen.

Use these options to maintain a distraction-free environment during transition periods between tasks.

## Whiteboard drawing tools

Once the whiteboard is activated, the Drawing Tools are visible to both you and your student. These drawing tools are designed to foster collaboration between you and your student. For many tools, additional style options (such as size, color, or transparency) will appear on the **right side** of the whiteboard when the tool is selected.

### **Managing Student Interaction Settings**

You can control student access to drawing tools through the participant options menu (see [Participant video display](/providers/participant-video-display) for additional details). This ensures you maintain full control over the session while students follow along what is on the whiteboard.

To prevent students from interacting with the whiteboard drawing tools, choose the  **Prevent mouse clicking in workspace** option from the participant options menu. This will allow you to still use the drawing tools while preventing students from modifying or changing what is on screen until you are done. Choosing **Allow mouse clicking in workspace** will allow students to interact with the whiteboard drawing tools again.

### Select tool

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/6a7884fd576990b50fa11394bd33f9c4dfb04179/images/Button%20-%20Select%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The select tool allows you to click any object on the whiteboard to be moved or modified. Once an object has been selected, the tool options will be displayed. For example, if you selected a shape, the options for that shape will be displayed to change its style.

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/4dda3dbc1bcc1f4919b4d1b0b9ab4023ea1d4415/images/Interface%20-%20Select%20tool%20options.svg" width="164" height="84" alt="" style="width: 124; height: 44; float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

When you click on an item using the select tool, a set of extra buttons will appear just below that item. You can use them to:

- **Duplicate** - Create a copy of the item.

- **Stack** - Place the item in front of or behind other items.

- **Remove** - Delete the selected item from the screen.

<Tabs>

### Pen

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Pen%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The pen tool lets you draw freehand lines or shapes on the board. It is perfect for writing, sketching, or underlining text. You can customize how the pen looks before you start drawing.

- **Color** - Pick any color you like for your lines.

- **Style** - Choose between a solid line, dashed lines, or dotted lines.

- **Size** - Make the line thick or thin to match your needs.

- **Transparency** - You can choose to make the line see-through (faded) or fully visible.

To change these settings, click on the pen tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Shapes

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/2cb87bd937f753e29a3aa0aa189d5e1b4063c4f4/images/Button%20-%20Shapes%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0px 0px 15px 15px;" />

The shapes tool allows you to add squares, rectangles, lines, ellipses/circles, triangles, or star shapes onto the whiteboard. Once a shape is selected, you can change how it looks.&#x20;

- **Fill color** - Choose the color for the inside of the shape.

- **Outline color** - Pick the color for the border around the shape.

- **Border size:&#xA0;**&#x4D;ake the outline thick or thin.

- **Border style:&#xA0;**&#x53;elect solid, dashed, or dotted lines for the outline.

To change these settings, click on the shapes tool in the toolbar, select a shape, and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Arrow

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Arrow%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The arrow tool lets you add a line with an arrow onto the whiteboard. It is perfect for pointing at specific areas of text or drawings. You can customize your arrows to match your needs:

- **Color** - Pick any color you like for the arrow.

- **See-through** - You can make the arrow fade away or fully visible.

- **Size** - Make the line thick or thin.

- **Style** - Choose between a solid line, dashed lines, or dotted lines.

To change these settings, click on the arrow tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Text

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Text%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The text tool lets you type words or sentences directly onto the whiteboard. It is great for adding notes, labels, or your own handwriting-style comments. You can customize how your text looks to help it stand out.

- **Text color** - Pick any color for the letters themselves.

- **See-through text** - Make the words fade in or look fully opaque.

- **Outline options** - Change the color and thickness (width) of the border around your text.

- **Letter size** - Make the text big or small.

- **Font style** - Choose how the letters look (e.g., standard, handwriting).

- **Text emphasis** - Bold, italicize, or underline your writing to make it stand out.

To change these settings, click on the text tool in the toolbar and then look at the menu bar on the right side of the screen for more options.

### Stamps

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Stamps%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The stamps tool allows you to place emoji style stamps onto the whiteboard. Think of stamps as shortcuts to add meaning or feelings to the whiteboard. You can find icons in the following categories:

- Activities

- Animals and nature

- Flags

- Animals

- Food and drink

- Objects

- People and body

- Smiley and emotion

- Symbols

- Travel and places

Once you pick a stamp, click on it to select it again if you want to move or change it (see the select tool above for additional details). You can also adjust how see-through the image is.&#x20;

### Erase

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/11f412c3929f5dee076cb73f84c474eb5164f52d/images/Button%20-%20Eraser%20tool.svg" width="45" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

The erase tool helps you clean up your work by removing specific parts of the whiteboard. You can erase individual lines, shapes, stamps, or text without losing everything.&#x20;

If you want to start with a fresh blank page, see the sheets tool above for additional details.

### Undo and redo

<Image src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/septherapy/help-center-documentation/4dda3dbc1bcc1f4919b4d1b0b9ab4023ea1d4415/images/Button%20-%20Undo%20and%20redo%20tool.svg" width="90" height="45" alt="" style="float: right; margin: 0 0 15px 15px;" />

These tools help you fix minor mistakes by allowing you to step back or step forward in what is on the whiteboard.

- **Undo** - Click this if you want to go back and remove something you added or changed.

- **Redo** - Use this to put an item back that you just undid. It is useful if you changed your mind about a change you made.
