---
title: Student records
---
Student records are used for tracking sessions and selecting assessments. When a student requests to be admitted to your therapy room, you select their student record in order to begin tracking that session. While you can create new student records in your therapy room when a student requests to be admitted, we recommend adding your caseload ahead of time.

## Accessing student records

To access your student records, click on the **Student records** link in the main navigation. On this page, you will see all of the student records you have created for the platform. Student records are not shared between providers and only you will be able to see the student records that you have created.

While you will be able to see all of your student records on the Student records page, only student records set to active will appear in the list of student records you can link to incoming participants in the therapy room (see [Admitting students](/providers/inviting-and-admitting-students) for more details).

<Callout kind="tip" collapsed="false">
  If you know you will no longer be seeing a student, we recommend you set their student record to inactive. You will still have access to their session history and can set the student back to active to have it appear in the therapy room again.
</Callout>

## Adding new student records

To add a new student record, click on the **Add new student record** button in the upper right hand corner. Fill out the student's name, date of birth, and district.

<Callout kind="info" collapsed="false">
  If you do not see a district you are working with, please contact [support@septherapy.com](mailto:support@septherapy.com).
</Callout>

Click the **Create new student** when you are done. The newly created record will show up in the table of all student records you have created.

### Adding new student records in the therapy room

You can also add a new student record in the therapy room when admitting a new participant (see [Admitting students](/providers/inviting-and-admitting-students) for more details). This is helpful in case you are meeting with a new student and did not have time to create a student record ahead of time.

## Editing student records

To edit a student record, click on the three dots menu icon in the actions column of the student records table and select **Edit**. You can also click on the **Edit student record** button while viewing any individual student record.

## Viewing student records

To view a student record, click on the three dots menu icon in the actions column of the student records table and select **View details**.

Below the basic details for the student record, two additional tabs allow you to see the most recent sessions this student participated in as well as any assessments that have been selected for this student.
