---
title: Participant video display
---
The layout of the therapy room is made up of the participant video display. All participants are represented by a video display box which shows the video feed from the primary camera if turned on. Document cameras are also represented by video display boxes if turned on by the participant. The participant's name is always displayed in the lower left hand corner.

By default, video display boxes adjust to maintain an equal ratio for all participants. The layout can be modified by increasing or decreasing individual video display boxes from the participant options menu.

---

## Managing your own video display

You can customize how your own video appears to others by clicking the options menu in the upper right-hand corner of your video display box.

- **Increase display size** - Enlarges your video display box and shrinks the other participants' video display boxes so you can bring focus to yourself or a document.

- **Decrease display size** - Shrinks your video display box to make more room for other video display boxes.

- **Rotate display** - Rotates your video 90 degrees to the right. You can click this multiple times to rotate through different angles.

- **Mirror display** - Flips your video horizontally. This is helpful if you are holding up text or a book that appears backward on screen.

- **Change display label** - Allows you to rename the label for your video display box. By default, this uses the name from your user profile. To change your name permanently, please update your user profile settings. If a document camera is active, the document camera will inherit the display name.

---

## Managing participant video displays

As the provider, you have advanced controls to manage how participants appear in your session. Access these by clicking the **options menu** on a participant's video display box.

### Audio and session controls

- **Request mute/unmute** - Sends a notification to the participant asking them to either mute or unmute their microphone.

- **Dismiss participant** - Removes the participant from the current session. For more information, see our guide on [Ending Sessions](/providers/ending-sessions).

### Visual management

You can modify how a student's video appears in your view using these tools:

- **Hide/show display** - You can hide a student's video display box from their view to reduce distractions while still keeping them in the session. The student will receive a notification that their display box has been hidden but their camera is still on so they are aware of the change.

- **Increase/decrease Size** - Just as with your own video, you can enlarge a student’s video display box to see them more clearly or shrink it to create more space.

- **Rotate** - Rotates the video 90 degrees to the right. Videos can be rotated multiple times.

- **Mirror** - You can flip a participant's video (useful if they are holding up a document) to make it easier for you to view.

- **Change display label** - Allows you to rename a student’s video display box for the duration of the session.

---

## Participant controls

Students have limited controls to ensure they can manage their own experience without disrupting the session. Students may:

- **Rotate display** - Rotates the display 90 degrees to the right. Videos can be rotated multiple times.

- **Mirror display** - Flips the display of the video. Helpful for when displaying text that may display backwards on screen.

- **Change display label** - Updates the name for their video display box for the duration of the session. If a document camera is active, the document camera will inherit the display name.
