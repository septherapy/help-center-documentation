---
title: Hand raising
---
The **Raise hand** button gives all participants a quiet way to signal that you want to speak, ask something, or take a turn. Instead of interrupting the conversation or typing a message, the action can be raise your hand and an icon appears on your video thumbnail so everyone can see it. Any participant in the therapy room can raise their hand. — providers and students alike. Everyone in the session sees the raised-hand icon on the thumbnail of whoever raised it.

## Raising and lowering your hand

To raise your hand, click the **Raise hand&#x20;**&#x62;utton in the bottom toolbar. Your video thumbnail will be highlighted in green and a raised hand icon will  appear near your name. The icon will stay visible until you lower your hand so you don't need to hold the button or repeat the action. The label on the button will change to **Lower hand**.

To lower your hand, click the **Lower hand** button. The icon disappears from your thumbnail and the button reverts to **Raise hand**. You can raise and lower your hand as many times as you like during a session.

---

## Provider controls

Providers can lower a student's hand on the student's behalf by selecting the Lower participants hand from the more options menu on any student's video thumbnail box.

The icon disappears from the student's thumbnail and their button returns to **Raise hand**. This is handy at the end of a turn-taking round or if a student forgot to lower their hand.
