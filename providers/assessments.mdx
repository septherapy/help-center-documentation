## Browsing assessments

Assessments available to you are listed in the Assessment directory in the navigation. The details page for each assessment provides useful information about the assessment including how the assessment will be presented in the therapy room as well as access to administrative materials such as manuals and scoring guides.

## Assigning an assessment&#x20;

Assessments are assigned to students on student records (see [Student records](/providers/student-records) for more details). From a student record, click on the Assessments tab. Here you can see any assessments that you have assigned to this student. To assign a new one, click the **Add assessment** button.

A modal will show you the assessments available to this student through their district. After you have selected an assessment, you will be shown the additional testing materials required for the assessment. Testing materials are available either as a digital download directly on the SEP Therapy Platform or from the district itself. It is your responsibility to ensure all testing materials are secured before administering the assessment to the student.

After you have confirmed your assessment choice and reviewed the testing materials, the assessment will be added to the student record. This table will show you when you added the assessment, when it was administered, and the details regarding testing materials (including the download links for materials provided by SEP.

## Administering assessments

Assessments are administered in the therapy room. To access an assessment you have assigned to a student, admit the student and select their student record. Once that is complete, click on the **Activities** button to open the activities panel and then click on the **Assessments** option. This will list all the assessments assigned to this particular student. Clicking on any assessment will allow you to launch the assessment. Once an assessment has been launched, it will be visible for both you and the student.

### Assessment instructions

Only you will be able to see the instructions in the right hand panel. An icon at the top of the instructions panel opens the same content in a small floating window which allows you to close the panel on the right for more space. The transparency of the overlay can be adjusted to reveal more or less of the stimulus. &#x20;

### Navigation

You have two ways to move through the assessment. The dropdown at the top of the right-hand panel lists every page by name (for example, a subtest's instructions, its demonstration/trial items, and each numbered item), and selecting an entry jumps straight to it. Alternatively, the page arrows next to the page counter (which reads "Page X of Y") move one page at a time in sequence, forward or backward.&#x20;

### **Additional controls**

- **Timers: &#x20;**&#x53;ome items include a built-in timer which can be used to track a student's response&#x20;
- **Audio prompts:&#x20;**&#x43;ertain items include audio that must be played for the student. Controls allow you to play the audio for you and the student.&#x20;
- **Audio recording:** To help with scoring, some prompts include the ability to record an audio response from the student. You can save these files to your computer.
