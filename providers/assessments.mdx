## Browsing assessments

Assessments available to you are listed in the Assessment directory in the navigation. The details page for each assessment provides useful information about the assessment including how the assessment will be presented in the therapy room as well as access to administrative materials such as manuals and scoring guides.

## Assigning an assessment

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the student's record" title-type="h2">
    Assessments are assigned to students on student records (see [Student records](/providers/student-records) for more details). From the student's record, click on the **Assessments** tab to see any assessments already assigned to this student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add an assessment" title-type="h2">
    Click the **Add assessment** button. A modal will show you the assessments available to this student through their district.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the assessment" title-type="h2">
    Choose the assessment you'd like to assign from the modal.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and secure testing materials" title-type="h2">
    You'll be shown the additional testing materials required for the assessment. These are available either as a digital download directly on the SEP Therapy Platform or from the district itself. It is your responsibility to ensure all testing materials are secured before administering the assessment to the student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the assessment" title-type="h2">
    Once you confirm your choice, the assessment is added to the student record. From there, you can see when you added the assessment, when it was administered, and details on testing materials, including download links for any materials provided by SEP.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Administering assessments

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admit the student" title-type="h2">
    Admit the student to your therapy room and select their student record.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the Assessments option" title-type="h2">
    Click the **Activities** button to open the activities panel, then click the **Assessments** option. This lists all the assessments assigned to this student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Launch the assessment" title-type="h2">
    Click on any assessment to launch it. Once launched, it will be visible for both you and the student.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Assessment instructions

Only you will be able to see the instructions in the right hand panel. An icon at the top of the instructions panel opens the same content in a small floating window which allows you to close the panel on the right for more space. The transparency of the overlay can be adjusted to reveal more or less of the stimulus.

### Navigation

You have two ways to move through the assessment. The dropdown at the top of the right-hand panel lists every page by name (for example, a subtest's instructions, its demonstration/trial items, and each numbered item), and selecting an entry jumps straight to it. Alternatively, the page arrows next to the page counter (which reads "Page X of Y") move one page at a time in sequence, forward or backward.

### Additional controls

- **Timers:** Some items include a built-in timer which can be used to track a student's response.
- **Audio prompts:** Certain items include audio that must be played for the student. Controls allow you to play the audio for you and the student.
- **Audio recording:** To help with scoring, some prompts include the ability to record an audio response from the student. You can save these files to your computer.
