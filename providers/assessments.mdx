## Browsing assessments

Assessments available to your discipline are listed in the Assessment directory. The details page for each assessment provides information on how the assessment will be presented in the therapy room. Assessments are either displayed with instructions or are accessed through the publisher's platform. The details page also includes links to administrative materials such as manuals and scoring guides are also available.

## Assigning assessments

In order to use an assessment with a student, you must first assign the assessment to them on their student record (see [Student records](/providers/student-records) for more details).&#x20;

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the student's record" title-type="h3">
    From the student's record, click on the **Assessments** tab to see any assessments already assigned to this student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Add an assessment" title-type="h3">
    Click the **Add assessment** button. A modal will show you the assessments available to this student through their district. Choose the assessment you'd like to assign to the student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Review and secure testing materials" title-type="h3">
    Once an assessment has been chosen, you will be shown additional testing materials required. Testing materials are available either as a digital download directly on the student's record or from the district itself. It is your responsibility to ensure all testing materials are secured before administering the assessment to the student.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Confirm the assessment" title-type="h3">
    Once you confirm your choice, the assessment is added to the student record. Each assessment assigned to a student will include the date the assessment was assigned, the date it was administered, and access instructions or links to testing materials.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Administering assessments

Assessments are administered in the therapy room and will either be presented directly in the room with instructions or will need to be accessed through the publisher's platform. Regardless of how the assessment is presented in the room, the method for administering them is the same.&#x20;

<Steps>
  <Step title="Admit the student" title-type="h3">
    Admit the student to your therapy room and select their student record. This will make sure that only assessments assigned to that student will be shown during the session.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Open the assessments activity" title-type="h3">
    Click the **Activities** button to open the activities panel and then click the **Assessments** option. This will list all the assessments assigned to this student.&#x20;
  </Step>

  <Step title="Select the assessment" title-type="h3">
    Click the assessment to select it. Additional pre-administration instructions will be displayed.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Begin the assessment" title-type="h3">
    When you're ready, click the **Begin assessment** button. Depending on how the assessment is displayed in the therapy room, you will either be able to start right away or will need to access the stimulus from the publisher's platform.&#x20;
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Assessments on publisher platform

Assessments available on the publisher's platform require additional setup before you can begin administration. Typically this involves accessing the record forms and stimulus books from the publisher's platform. Stimulus books will need to be screen shared in order for students to see the content.&#x20;

[Working with PAR assessments](/providers/par-assessments)&#x20;

## Assessments with embedded instructions

Assessments with embedded instructions display the stimulus in the therapy room with instructions in the panel on the right side. &#x20;

The dropdown at the top of the panel lists every page by name (for example, a subtest's instructions, its demonstration/trial items, and each numbered item), and selecting an entry jumps straight to it. Alternatively, the page arrows next to the page counter (which reads "Page X of Y") move one page at a time in sequence, forward or backward.

Only you will be able to see the instructions. An icon at the top of the instructions panel opens the same content in a small floating window which allows you to close the panel on the right for more space. The transparency of the overlay can be adjusted to reveal more or less of the stimulus.

### Additional controls

- **Timers:** Some items include a built-in timer which can be used to track a student's response.
- **Audio prompts:** Certain items include audio that must be played for the student. Controls allow you to play the audio for you and the student.
- **Audio recording:** To help with scoring, some prompts include the ability to record an audio response from the student. You can save these files to your computer.
