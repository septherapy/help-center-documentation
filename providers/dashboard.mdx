---
title: Dashboard
---
After you sign in to the platform, you will be redirected to your dashboard. From here, you will be able to find your unique link for inviting participants to your therapy room, your waiting room notifications, and links to help and support resources.

- **Your therapy room invitation link** - You will see your unique room link prominently displayed. From here, you can copy the link or send invitations via email directly through the interface. To access your therapy room, look for the button under the link or navigate via the menu on the left.

- **Your waiting room notification area** - Just to the right of the therapy room invitation section, you will find your waiting room. This is where you’ll be notified when a student requests to join a session. Their details will appear here as they are waiting.

- **Help and support resources** - If you need help interacting with any features, please check the resources listed at the bottom of the screen.
