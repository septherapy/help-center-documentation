These resources are designed to help you get your school setup for working with the Strategic Education Partners (SEP) Therapy Platform. The SEP Therapy Platform allows SEP providers to conduct virtual one-on-one and group sessions with students at your schools.    

This guide will help you set up students for successful teletherapy sessions using our video conferencing platform. Whether a session is taking place at school or at home, a little preparation goes a long way toward making the experience comfortable and productive for everyone involved.

## Supported devices

Students must use a laptop or desktop computer. Phones and tablets are not supported. We recommend laptops for their built-in camera, microphone, and speakers, which keeps the setup simple without needing extra accessories.
