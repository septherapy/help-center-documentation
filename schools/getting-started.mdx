title: Getting started
---
This guide will help you set up students for successful teletherapy sessions using the SEP Therapy Platform. Whether a session is taking place at school or at home, a little preparation goes a long way toward making the experience comfortable and productive for everyone involved.

We recommend you review the entire setup guide but here's a quick overview to get started.

<Columns cols="3">
  <Card title="Devices" icon="lucide-laptop-minimal-check" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    Students should use a laptop or desktop computer. Phones and tablets aren't supported. Laptops are our recommendation since they have a built-in camera, microphone, and speakers.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Browsers" icon="chrome" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    Students access the platform through a web browser. We support the latest versions of Safari, Chrome, Edge, and Firefox on Apple macOS and Microsoft Windows.&#x20;
  </Card>

  <Card title="Internet" icon="lucide-network" horizontal="false" target="_self">
    A wired Ethernet connection is strongly preferred for its reliability and stability. Wi-Fi is acceptable if a wired connection isn't available, but hotspots and mobile data should be avoided.
  </Card>
</Columns>
