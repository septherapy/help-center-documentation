---
title: Firewall settings
---
The SEP Therapy Platform is accessed entirely through a web browser with no additional software installation required. However, to ensure students can connect successfully, your network firewall must be configured to allow traffic on specific ports. Please review the port requirements in the table below and ensure these are opened before any student sessions are scheduled. 

| Protocol | Ports | Destination                         | Explanation          |
| -------- | ----- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.septherapy.com                   | general access       |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.firebaseio.com \*.googleapis.com | teletherapy services |
