title: Setup guide

When students are joining sessions from school, the school is responsible for providing an appropriate space and ensuring the technology is ready to go.

Students should have access to a private or semi-private space for their session. This doesn’t need to be a dedicated therapy room — a quiet corner of a library, a small meeting room, or a counselor’s office can all work well.

- Minimize background noise and interruptions

- Allow the student to speak without being easily overheard

- Have enough lighting so the student’s face is clearly visible on camera

- Be free from distracting backgrounds where possible

## Supported devices

Students must use a laptop or desktop computer. Phones and tablets are not supported. We recommend laptops for their built-in camera, microphone, and speakers, which keeps the setup simple without needing extra accessories.

## Supported browsers

Our platform supports the latest versions of <a href="https://www.apple.com/safari/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Apple Safari</a>, <a href="https://www.google.com/chrome/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Google Chrome</a>, <a href="https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/edge/download" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Microsoft Edge</a>, and [Mozilla Firefox](https://www.firefox.com). Note that Safari is only available on macOS.

| Operating system          | Apple Safari | Google Chrome | Microsoft Edge | Mozilla Firefox |
| ------------------------- | ------------ | ------------- | -------------- | --------------- |
| Apple macOS 15 (Sequoia)  | ✅            | ✅             | ✅              | ✅               |
| Apple macOS 15 (Sequoia)  | ✅            | ✅             | ✅              | ✅               |
| Microsoft Windows 10      | ⛔            | ✅             | ✅              | ✅               |
| Microsoft Windows 11      | ⛔            | ✅             | ✅              | ✅               |

Always use the most current version of a supported browser. Older versions may not connect reliably or may be missing security updates. Most browsers update automatically but it’s worth confirming before a student’s first session.

## Internet connection

A wired (Ethernet) connection is strongly preferred over Wi-Fi. Wired connections are more stable and less likely to drop during a session which matters especially for therapy where continuity and privacy are important. If a wired connection isn’t available, a strong and stable Wi-Fi signal is acceptable. Avoid using a hotspot or mobile data if possible.

## Firewall configuration

In order for your students to access the SEP Therapy Platform, the following ports will need to be configured.

| Protocol | Ports | Destination                         | Explanation          |
| -------- | ----- | ----------------------------------- | -------------------- |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.septherapy.com                   | general access       |
| TCP      | 443   | \*.firebaseio.com \*.googleapis.com | teletherapy services |
