title: Setup guide
---
The SEP Therapy Platform is accessed by students through a standard web browser. Students should have access to a private or semi-private space for their session. This doesn’t need to be a dedicated therapy room but can be a quiet corner of a library, a small meeting room, or a counselor’s office. If students are joining sessions from home, parents or guardians play an important role in making sure the environment and technology are ready.

When finding a suitable space for students, keep in mind the following.

- Minimize background noise and interruptions

- Allow the student to speak without being easily overheard

- Have enough lighting so the student’s face is clearly visible on camera

- Be free from distracting backgrounds where possible

## **Evaluations and assessments**

If a student is participating in a formal evaluation or assessment rather than a standard therapy session, additional requirements apply. Please make sure these are in place before the appointment. A noisy or shared space can compromise the integrity of the evaluation.

Students must be in a quiet fully private space for any evaluation or assessment. Semi-private or open areas are not appropriate. Background noise, interruptions, or the presence of others can interfere with the process and may require the session to be rescheduled. 

### **Document camera**

Some evaluation activities require a document camera to allow the therapist to see physical materials (such as worksheets or manipulatives) that the student is working with. If a document camera is required for an upcoming session, the therapist or coordinator will let you know in advance. Document cameras connect via USB and should be set up and tested before the session begins.

## Supported devices

Students must use a laptop or desktop computer. Phones and tablets are not supported. We recommend laptops for their built-in camera, microphone, and speakers, which keeps the setup simple without needing extra accessories.

While a laptop with built-in hardware is all that’s required, the following accessories are supported and can improve the experience:

- **Wired mouse (recommended)** makes navigating the platform easier than a laptop's trackpad

- **External webcam** if the built-in camera is low quality or blocked

- **External microphone** or **headset** helpful in noisier environments

- **Headphones** can help with focus and audio clarity

## Internet connection

A wired (Ethernet) connection is strongly preferred over Wi-Fi. Wired connections are more stable and less likely to drop during a session which matters especially for therapy where continuity and privacy are important. If a wired connection isn’t available, a strong and stable Wi-Fi signal is acceptable. Avoid using a hotspot or mobile data if possible.
